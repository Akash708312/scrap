# 🎂 Siddhi's Magical Birthday Website 💕

A super romantic and interactive birthday experience with tons of love, kisses, and animations!

## ✨ Features

### 🎨 Continuous Animations
- **Kiss Rain** 💋 - Continuous kisses falling from the sky
- **Rose Petals** 🌹 - Beautiful flower petals floating down
- **Floating Hearts** 💕 - Hearts rising up in the background
- **Sparkle Trail** ✨ - Magical sparkles follow your cursor everywhere

### 🎭 9 Interactive Scenes

1. **Opening Scene** - Animated name reveal with starry background
2. **Love Lock** 🔐 - Click to unlock and reveal a romantic message with kiss explosion
3. **Kiss Collector Game** 💋 - Catch 20 flying kisses! Interactive and fun!
4. **Message in Bottles** 💌 - Click bottles to read 5 romantic messages
5. **Memory Carousel** 📸 - 3D rotating carousel with your photos
6. **Birthday Cake** 🎂 - Blow out the candles one by one
7. **Heart Photo Collage** 💗 - Photos arranged in a beating heart shape
8. **Love Letter** 💌 - Click envelope to read a heartfelt letter
9. **Final Surprise** 🎁 - Click the gift box for promises and massive confetti celebration!

## 📸 Adding Your Photos

Replace these placeholder files with your actual photos:

- `photo1.jpg` - Your first memory together
- `photo2.jpg` - Second special moment
- `photo3.jpg` - Third cherished memory
- `photo4.jpg` - Fourth beautiful moment

Place all photos in the same folder as `index.html`.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Add Photos**: Replace `photo1.jpg` through `photo4.jpg` with your real photos
2. **Open in Browser**: Double-click `index.html` to open in your browser
3. **Share with Siddhi**: Send her the folder or host it online!

## 💝 Customization Tips

### Change Colors
Edit the `styles.css` file to modify:
- Background gradients
- Button colors
- Text colors

### Modify Messages
Edit `index.html` to change:
- Bottle messages (Scene 4)
- Love letter content (Scene 8)
- Final promises (Scene 9)

### Adjust Animations
Edit `script.js` to control:
- Kiss rain frequency
- Petal falling speed
- Heart floating rate

## 🎵 Adding Background Music (Optional)

Add this code in the `<body>` section of `index.html`:

```html
<audio autoplay loop>
    <source src="romantic-song.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
</audio>
```
